Step 4: Choose Assessment Type

Please select the assessment length that works best for you:

⚡ QUICK PIT Assessment

Duration: 2-3 minutes
Questions: 7 questions
Sections: 3 sections

Ultra-fast count with essential information only: name, gender, age/DOB, current housing situation, and consent.

Best for: Rapid PIT count enumeration

📝 SHORT Assessment

Duration: 6-10 minutes
Questions: ~25 questions
Sections: 7 sections

Quick count covering essential information: current location, duration of homelessness, basic demographics, and service needs.

Best for: Rapid enumeration and basic service planning

📋 MEDIUM Assessment

Duration: 12-20 minutes
Questions: 50-70 questions
Sections: 10 sections

Balanced assessment including housing history, health and wellness, income, barriers to housing, and detailed service needs.

Best for: Comprehensive service planning and resource allocation

📊 HARD Assessment

Duration: 25-40 minutes
Questions: 100-120 questions
Sections: 12 sections

Detailed, high-resolution survey covering full housing timeline, comprehensive health assessment, justice involvement, and detailed service matching.

Best for: Individual case planning and targeted intervention strategies
