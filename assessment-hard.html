HARD Assessment - Comprehensive Survey

This is a detailed, high-resolution assessment with 100-120 questions across 12 sections covering:

  • Screening and Consent (with digital signature capability)
  • Demographics (comprehensive including sexual orientation, languages, immigration)
  • Household Composition (including custody and family connections)
  • Housing History (full timeline of last 5 residences)
  • Current Living Situation (detailed safety and amenity assessment)
  • Health and Wellness (physical, mental health, substance use, pain scale)
  • Income and Employment (including informal income, education, ID possession)
  • Service Connections (detailed agency relationships, service ratings)
  • Justice and Safety (police interactions, custody, victimization)
  • Technology and Communication (phone, internet, contact preferences)
  • Goals and Supports (priorities, housing readiness, barriers)
  • Enumerator's Observation (visual indicators, safety notes)

High-Resolution Features:

  • Auto-validation and skip logic
  • GPS coordinates and timestamp capture
  • Optional voice-note summary
  • Real-time risk scoring
  • Automated referral export capability

Note: The full HARD assessment form follows the same structure as the SHORT assessment but with comprehensive detailed questions in each section plus additional specialized sections. All questions include "Prefer not to say" or "Skip" options.
