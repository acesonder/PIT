MEDIUM Assessment Form

This is a balanced, planning-grade assessment with 50-70 questions across 10 sections covering:

  • Screening & Consent
  • Current Location & Situation
  • Homelessness History
  • Demographics
  • Household & Dependents
  • Health & Well-Being
  • Income & Employment
  • Services & Supports
  • Justice & Safety
  • Location & Closure

Note: The full MEDIUM assessment form follows the same structure as the SHORT assessment but with additional detailed questions in each section. All questions include "Prefer not to say" or "Skip" options.

This form would include all questions listed in the MEDIUM PIT ASSESSMENT section of the requirements, properly formatted with form controls, radio buttons, checkboxes, and dropdowns.
